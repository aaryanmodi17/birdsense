# FIGURE_TABLE_PLAN.md

Version 2.0 — Scoped Edition (9 figures, 6 tables — down from 15+15)

## Purpose

Every figure and table the paper needs. Trimmed to what a focused
research paper actually uses, not what a full platform's report engine
would generate.

---

## Figures

**Figure 1 — Study Area Map**
Gujarat with key wetlands/hotspots (Nal Sarovar, Little Rann of Kutch,
others relevant to the researcher's own sightings) marked. Static map,
matplotlib/geopandas or a simple Folium export.

**Figure 2 — Observer Effort Growth (2010–2025)**
Annual complete checklists over time. This is the concrete 30x-growth
chart from the real data — do this one first, it's both easy and a
strong opening figure for the Methods section.

**Figure 3 — Raw vs. Confirmed Arrival Comparison**
For 2–3 example species: raw single-earliest-record arrival vs. the
2nd-observation confirmed arrival, side by side per year. Demonstrates
why the correction matters — this is the figure that shows your
methodology isn't arbitrary.

**Figure 4 — Arrival Date Trend, All Species**
Small-multiples grid (12 subplots) or overlaid lines: confirmed arrival
date by year, with trend line, per species.

**Figure 5 — Departure Date Trend, All Species**
Same structure as Figure 4, for departure.

**Figure 6 — Temperature vs. Arrival Date (H1)**
Scatter plot with regression line, one panel per species or a faceted
grid, temperature on x-axis, arrival date on y-axis.

**Figure 7 — Habitat Category Comparison (H3)**
The core original-contribution figure: average trend line for
wetland-dependent species vs. grassland/dryland species, overlaid.

**Figure 8 — Geographic Centroid Shift**
Map or scatter showing annual centroid position, colored by year, for
2–3 focal species.

**Figure 9 — Dalmatian Pelican Case Study**
Timeline of all available sightings (not just confirmed years) —
qualitative, not a trend line. Include a short narrative panel.

## Tables

**Table 1 — Study Species**
Common name, scientific name, habitat category, total confirmed
species-years (out of 16).

**Table 2 — Data Sources**
Dataset, provider, years covered, resolution, cost ($0 for all).

**Table 3 — Data Quality Summary**
Total checklists, dropped by rule (Historical, pre-2010, invalid
coordinates), final usable count, per-species confirmed-year counts.
Pulls directly from the Validation Report in
`05_DATA_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`.

**Table 4 — Migration Metrics Summary**
Per species: mean arrival date, trend slope (days/year), p-value, mean
departure date, trend slope, p-value.

**Table 5 — Correlation Results (H1)**
Per species: temperature-arrival correlation coefficient, p-value,
interpretation category (strong/weak/none).

**Table 6 — Hypothesis Evaluation**
H1, H2, H3: evidence summary, decision (Supported/Not
Supported/Inconclusive), one-line interpretation. This is the table an
a reader will look at first — make it clear and
honest.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every figure/table is generated by running the notebook top to
  bottom — no manually-edited numbers.
- Every figure has units, a legend, and a caption stating the data
  source and study period.
- Table 6 is written last, after all other analysis is complete, and
  is not softened to look more conclusive than the actual results
  support.
